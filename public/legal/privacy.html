Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-30 · Last updated: 2026-06-30

1. Who we are

Orbita is an experimental research platform operated by Derek Earnhart ("we," "our," "us"). Contact: derekearnhart1@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: email address, username, hashed password, account creation date, last login.
  • Uploaded data: CSV files you upload for discovery analysis. These are stored on our infrastructure for the duration of a case.
  • Usage data: IP address, user agent, timestamps of actions, audit logs of key events (signup, login, file upload, run started).
  • Session data: encrypted session identifiers stored in PostgreSQL.

3. How we use your data

  • To operate the Orbita service and run discovery analyses on your uploaded datasets.
  • To send account-related emails (email verification, password reset).
  • To enforce usage quotas and detect abuse.
  • To maintain security audit logs.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

4. Data storage and retention

Data is stored on Railway.app infrastructure. We retain account data while your account is active. Uploaded CSV files and discovery artifacts are retained until you delete the case or your account. Audit logs are retained for up to 90 days.

5. Data deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Account page. Deletion is immediate and permanent. See our Data Deletion page for details.

6. Cookies and sessions

We use a single session cookie (orbita.sid) to maintain your login. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

7. Third-party services

  • Railway.app: Infrastructure hosting (EU/US).
  • Resend: Transactional email delivery.
  • Cloudflare: CAPTCHA verification on sign-up and login forms.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing derekearnhart1@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

We will notify registered users by email of material changes to this policy. Continued use after notice constitutes acceptance.
