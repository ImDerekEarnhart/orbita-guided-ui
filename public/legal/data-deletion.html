Data Deletion

Last updated: 2026-06-30

You have the right to delete your Orbita account and all associated data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record (email, username, password hash)
  • All discovery cases and their associated files
  • All uploaded CSV datasets
  • All discovery plans, run results, and evidence artifacts
  • Your usage quota and run history
  • Pending email verification and password reset tokens

Security audit logs (IP address, timestamps of key events) are retained for up to 90 days as required for abuse prevention, then permanently deleted.

Self-service deletion (instant)

1
Sign in to your account

Go to orbita.app/login and sign in.

2
Open Account settings

Click your username in the navigation bar and select Account.

3
Click "Delete my account"

Scroll to the bottom of the Account page and click the red Delete button.

4
Confirm with your password

Enter your password to confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Manual deletion request

If you cannot access your account, email derekearnhart1@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your registered email address. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Individual case deletion

To delete a single discovery case without deleting your account, open the case from your case list and use the Delete case option. This removes the case, its uploaded file, and all associated results from both our platform database and the Orbita discovery engine.
