Experimental Research Disclaimer

Effective date: 2026-06-30

Orbita is experimental research software in public beta. Its findings are statistical patterns identified in your data — not verified scientific conclusions. Do not rely on Orbita findings alone to make important decisions.

What Orbita does

Orbita applies automated statistical and machine-learning methods to datasets you provide. It generates candidate relationships, challenges them against held-out data, and reports which structures survived falsification under the configured methodology.

What Orbita does not do

  • Orbita does not establish causation. Statistical association is not causation.
  • Orbita does not guarantee that findings will replicate on future data or different samples.
  • Orbita does not check whether your data is correctly collected, labeled, or representative.
  • Orbita does not validate the scientific or business significance of any finding.
  • Orbita does not replace domain expertise, peer review, or regulatory approval.

Limitations of the methodology

  • Results depend on the quality and completeness of data you provide.
  • The platform is under active development. Algorithms, parameters, and outputs may change between versions.
  • Statistical artifacts, data leakage, and spurious correlations are possible and may not be flagged automatically.
  • Small datasets may produce unreliable results.

Do not use Orbita findings as the sole basis for

  • Medical diagnosis, treatment, or clinical decisions.
  • Legal, financial, or investment decisions.
  • Safety-critical engineering or infrastructure decisions.
  • Regulatory filings or compliance determinations.
  • Publication without independent peer review.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for evaluating Orbita's output critically and for any decisions you make based on it. Always involve qualified domain experts and apply appropriate validation before acting on any finding.
