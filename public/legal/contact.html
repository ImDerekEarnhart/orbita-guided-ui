Security vulnerability reporting

Please report security issues privately

Do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities before we have had a chance to address them. Email details to derekearnhart1@gmail.com with the subject line "Security Report — Orbita".

When reporting a security issue, please include:

  • A clear description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue.
  • Any proof-of-concept code or screenshots (do not include real user data).
  • Your contact information if you would like acknowledgement.

We will acknowledge receipt within 2 business days and provide a remediation timeline. We will credit researchers who responsibly disclose issues (unless they prefer to remain anonymous).
